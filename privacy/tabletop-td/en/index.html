Possible data and services

The following describes potential handling only. A feature listed here does not mean its SDK is installed or collecting data.

If local saves are implemented, gameplay progress, scores and settings may be stored on the device.

If Google AdMob ads are enabled, Google may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic information according to its policies.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Advertising and choices

If ads are included, required consent and privacy options must be offered in the app for relevant regions. We plan to use Google's consent solution where applicable. This has not yet been confirmed in a release build.

Storage, deletion and rights

Data retention periods, international transfers and deletion methods are not yet confirmed. Clearing local app data may remove local saves but does not necessarily remove data held by other services. Contact us with privacy requests.

Children and age targeting

The game's final target age group and any child-specific protections will be confirmed before launch.

Before publishing

We must audit the final build and SDKs, confirm purposes and legal bases, data recipients, retention, privacy rights, age targeting, international transfers and legal contact details. The final policy, in-app links and Google Play Data safety declaration must all match actual behavior.
