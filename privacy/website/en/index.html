Contact

This site is published under the Noodlecoil Games brand. You can email noodlecoilgames@gmail.com with privacy questions.

This static site does not intentionally use analytics, ad scripts, tracking pixels, newsletter forms or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process IP addresses and technical request logs to operate and secure the hosting service.

Your rights and external links

You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. External sites have their own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement for GitHub Pages hosting details.

Changes
