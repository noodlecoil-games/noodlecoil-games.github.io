Website privacy

A straightforward overview of how this small, static website works.

Published 29 October 2026Applies to this website only

Who runs this website?

This site is published under the name Noodlecoil Games. For questions about this website's privacy practices, email noodlecoilgames@gmail.com.

Information we collect

This is a static website. We have not added advertising scripts, third-party analytics, newsletter forms, social media trackers or tracking cookies to its pages.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with other hosting services, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata needed to operate and secure the service. That processing is governed by GitHub's own policies.

When you contact us

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message and any information you include. We use that information to read and respond to your request, and to follow up where needed. Please avoid sending passwords, identification documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Sharing and storage

We do not run a marketing database or intentionally sell visitor data collected through this website. Email messages are handled by our email service provider, and website hosting is handled by GitHub. We retain correspondence as reasonably needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations.

Your choices

You can browse this website without registering an account. To ask about personal information you have shared with us by email, or to request correction or deletion where applicable, contact noodlecoilgames@gmail.com.

External links

Links to other websites or services are covered by those services' own policies, not this page. For details about GitHub's processing, consult the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Updates

If this website adds analytics, contact forms, advertising scripts or other data-processing features, this page will be updated to describe them.
