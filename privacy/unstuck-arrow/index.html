PRE-RELEASE DRAFT

Not yet a final store-ready privacy policy

Unstuck Arrow is in development. The details below describe features under consideration; they do not confirm what the released app actually collects or shares. This page must be reviewed against the final game build and SDK inventory before it is submitted to Google Play or AdMob.

Draft updated 29 October 2026Project: Unstuck Arrow

1. Who to contact

This project is developed under the brand Noodlecoil Games. For privacy questions about Unstuck Arrow, contact noodlecoilgames@gmail.com. Final legal-controller and business contact details will be confirmed before commercial release.

2. Features currently envisaged

  • Local puzzle progress and preferences
  • Potential rewarded advertisements for optional hints or assistance
  • No confirmed online account feature at this stage

3. Potential data handling

The table is a planning checklist, not a declaration that all listed features are active. The actual practices and precise purposes will be confirmed before release.

FeaturePossible informationCurrent status
Gameplay and settingsLevel progress, preferences and any hint usageExpected local game data; confirm in the finished app
Ads (if enabled)Device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnosticsPossible rewarded-ad integration; verify before release

4. Service providers

If implemented, Google AdMob may process information to deliver, measure and protect ads; Google Play Games Services may process player identity and leaderboard information; Google Play Billing may process purchase-related information. These services have their own privacy practices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising choices and consent

If advertising is included at release, the app's consent flow must be implemented and tested for relevant regions (including the EEA, UK and Switzerland). Where required, players will be given applicable consent and privacy choices inside the app. This website does not itself display an in-app consent form.

6. Your choices and requests

Local progress may be removable through the device's application-storage controls, but this does not necessarily delete data held by third-party services. For questions or requests relating to the finished game, email noodlecoilgames@gmail.com. Any account deletion, retention periods, international transfers and age-related requirements will be specified once the released features are confirmed.

7. Project-specific note

Whether the released game uses ads, analytics, cloud saves or sign-in is not yet confirmed. This draft should not be used as the final app-store privacy disclosure.

8. Before publication

We will replace this draft with a complete app-specific policy covering actual data categories, recipients, legal purposes and bases, retention, privacy rights, children's data practices and contact information, as applicable. The Google Play Data safety form must match the release build and its third-party SDKs.
