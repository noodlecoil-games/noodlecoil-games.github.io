Unstuck Arrow — privacy information

This is not the final store-ready privacy policy. The v1.17 code, libraries and manifest were inspected, but live Android SDK traffic, final AdMob partners, production service settings and the publisher's legal identity have not been verified. The final policy must be completed and reviewed before release.

Reviewed build: v1.17 (versionCode 27), 29 October 2026; pilot ads use test units.

Privacy information informed by the v1.17 Android audit.

1. Developer and contact

Unstuck Arrow is developed under the public name Noodlecoil Games. The legal publisher and data controller have not yet been finalized. For privacy questions or requests, write to noodlecoilgames@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on the device

The game saves progress, levels, mode and difficulty, tutorial completion, sound and language settings, hint credits and ad-frequency information locally. It also stores recent completed-run details (up to 80 summaries), scores, achievement-related counters, dated leaderboard totals and account-scoped upload checkpoints. A local event counter is not sent to a Noodlecoil analytics server. Some accumulated totals have no automatic expiry.

3. Consent choices (Google UMP)

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP 4.0.0) checks consent information at game launch and provides Google's consent and Privacy Options screens when required. Google may receive the app identifier, consent-related values, device model and operating-system details, language, screen characteristics and connection/IP information. Consent choices are stored by the SDK on the device. Changing preferences does not erase data already processed by Google; SDK components can initialize before an explicit ad request.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0 is integrated for interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. The inspected pilot/review builds use sample ad units; production advertising is not enabled. Ad loads and displays are gated by UMP's canRequestAds result. Depending on device, consent and configuration, Google's ad services and authorized ad partners may process advertising or app identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app/device details, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics or performance data. The game does not request precise GPS permission. Refusing personalized ads does not block gameplay and does not guarantee that every Google request stops; limited or non-personalized ads may still be possible.

5. Play Games leaderboards

Google Play Games Services v2 22.1.0 is included for optional account sign-in and an all-time cumulative leaderboard. It may process Google player identifiers, public display names, uploaded scores and ranking information; other players may see names, scores and ranks. SDK initialization or sign-in checks may happen automatically. Campaign progress and complete move histories are not uploaded as cloud saves. Live Play Games IDs were not configured in the audited pilot/review build.

6. Google Play Billing and Premium

Google Play Billing 8.3.0 is integrated for a possible non-consumable remove_ads purchase. When configured, Google handles payments; the game can receive purchase state, receipt or purchase-token references and cache verified entitlement information in private app storage. It does not receive full payment-card numbers. Ownership checks may run at startup or resume. Premium suppresses automatic interstitials, while optional rewarded ads can remain available. Production Billing was not configured in the audited builds.

7. Other providers, permissions and backups

No Noodlecoil-operated backend, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, camera, microphone, contact or precise-location permission was found. However, Google SDKs and their transitive measurement/diagnostic components are present; the absence of a custom analytics service does not mean no Google telemetry. Google services and ad partners may retain information under their own policies. Android backup is enabled: some preferences and SDK files may be backed up or restored; WebView progress and Billing receipt files are excluded by the app's explicit backup rules. Opening this policy also contacts GitHub Pages and your browser provider.

8. Your controls, retention and deletion

You can play without agreeing to personalized ads or signing in to Play Games. When required, Settings → Privacy Options opens Google's choices again. Android Settings → Apps → Unstuck Arrow → Storage → Clear data, or uninstalling, normally removes local app data; this does not delete Google Play Games leaderboard data, Google purchase records, advertising records or all possible cloud backups. Google Play Games data deletion, Google Play purchase controls and Android advertising privacy controls are separate. No in-game delete-all button or universal provider-deletion service was found. Contact noodlecoilgames@gmail.com with requests.

9. Children and age targeting

The audited configuration assumes a general audience, with no in-game age collection or age gate; the under-age flag is false by default unless a child-directed build is selected. Target age and any additional child-specific measures must be reviewed before release.

10. Items still to finalize before publication

Confirm the legal publisher/controller and contact details; target audience; actual AdMob message publication, partners and vendors; real Play Games/Billing configuration; actual native SDK traffic; purposes and lawful bases; retention periods, international transfers and user rights; and all applicable Play Console Data safety disclosures. Review the translations and ensure the final in-app policy link points to the reviewed notice.
